About the club

Good people. Great stories. A little dirt.

The McLean County Metal Detecting Club is a community for people who enjoy metal detecting and the history that can surface beneath our feet. Whether you’re an experienced detectorist or just curious about getting started, there’s always something new to learn.

Our aim is to make the hobby welcoming, educational, and respectful of the places we explore.

“The best find is the one that connects us to a story worth preserving.”
  • Learn from fellow hobbyists
  • Trade tips, techniques, and stories
  • Explore local history with care
  • Help keep the hobby welcoming

Metal detecting is about more than what turns up in the ground. It’s the people you meet, the skills you build, and the history you help bring to light.

Leave it better

Respect the land. Preserve the story.

Responsible detecting protects access to places we love and helps ensure the hobby can be enjoyed for years to come.

Ask About Club Guidelines

Our responsible detecting pledge

  • Get permission before detecting on private property.
  • Check local rules and obtain required permits.
  • Respect parks, protected sites, and no-dig areas.
  • Fill every hole and pack out litter you find.
  • Handle historic or potentially significant finds thoughtfully.
  • Be courteous to landowners and the public.
